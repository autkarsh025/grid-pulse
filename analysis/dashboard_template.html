grid-pulse

An automated pipeline that ingests hourly U.S. grid data from the EIA API, validates it, loads it into SQL and tracks how battery storage covers solar's evening drop-off.

The duck curve, and the batteries filling it

Average hour of the day over the last 90 days (local time). Batteries charge on midday solar (below zero) and discharge into the evening peak.

Grid carbon intensity by hour

Average kg of CO₂ per MWh generated, by local hour. Green dots mark when batteries charge, red when they discharge.

Generation mix

Monthly share of generation by fuel (batteries excluded: they store energy, they don't make it).

Monthly battery discharge

Energy sent to the grid by battery storage, per month.

Day-ahead forecast error

EIA demand forecast vs. actual, 7-day rolling MAPE.

Anomaly explorer

Last 30 days, hourly. Points are flagged by a robust z-score against the same hour on the prior 28 days (|z| ≥ 4), or as flatlines (a non-zero value stuck for 8+ hours).

Top-10 demand days

Battery output at each day's peak hour.

Pipeline health

Every run is logged; failed quality checks block the load.
